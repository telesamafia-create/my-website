01 / WHAT IT IS

Search SoundCloud for tracks, albums and playlists. Import public Spotify metadata and find a playable match. Read lyrics through LRCLIB. Keep favourites separate from the library, export the library as JSON, and move through queue, history, shuffle and repeat without leaving the keyboard.

03 / WINDOWS

An ordinary app,
not a browser tab.

An icon in the tray, playback controls in the tray, the current track in Discord. It stays tucked away until you call it, and it does not ask for an account.

04 / DETAILS

Everything stays
on your machine.

Your library, favourites, history and settings sit in the app folder on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account to create and no telemetry to opt out of. The source is open and builds for all three platforms are on GitHub.
